For Whom

Who Is It For?

Treasurio is a crypto management terminal built for professional traders who want to manage multiple exchanges from a single terminal, active investors tracking spot/futures positions and staking income, analysts monitoring whale movements and smart money flows, and teams requiring institutional-grade reporting.

Process

How It Works

Turn market data into action in three steps.

Step 01

Sign Up & Connect

Create your account and integrate your preferred exchanges. Setup completes in minutes.

Step 02

Analyze

Track real-time orderbook depth, whale movements, and exchange flow data — all in one screen.

Step 03

Decide

Make informed, fast trading decisions with AI-powered signals, risk metrics, and liquidity analysis.

Features

Everything You Need

Everything professional traders need, one platform.

Deep Liquidity Analysis

Real-time orderbook depth, spread analysis and market depth visualization across Binance, Bybit, OKX and 50+ exchanges.

Exchange Flow Tracking

Instantly detect cross-exchange fund flows, whale movements and large transfers. Spot market manipulation with smart algorithms.

Advanced Risk Management

Slippage calculation, market impact analysis, optimized execution strategies and portfolio risk metrics.

AI-Powered Analytics

Trade cost estimation, price trend analysis, anomaly detection and automated signal generation — all powered by machine learning.

Ultra-Low Latency

Millisecond-precision data streams via WebSocket. Co-location support for minimum latency.

RESTful & WebSocket API

Power your own bots and strategies with the Treasurio API. Comprehensive documentation and SDKs included.

Customizable Dashboard

Build your own workspace with drag & drop. Multi-monitor support, saved layouts and shareable views.

Enterprise Security

End-to-end encryption, 2FA, API key management and IP whitelist. SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure.

Multi-Platform

Web, Desktop (Windows/Mac/Linux) and mobile apps. Synchronized experience across all your devices.
